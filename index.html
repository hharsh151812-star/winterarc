DETAIL DATABASE

💧 Hydration

Keep fluids available throughout the day and respond to thirst.

Do

  • Keep a bottle nearby.
  • Drink regularly rather than relying on one large intake.

Don't

  • Force excessive water intake.
  • Use hydration as a replacement for meals.

😴 Sleep

Sleep is one of the Arc's recovery systems.

Do

  • Keep a reasonably consistent sleep schedule.
  • Reduce bright screens before bed.

Don't

  • Trade sleep for unnecessary late-night productivity.

🏋 Training

Build consistency with appropriate resistance and recovery.

Do

  • Use controlled technique.
  • Progress gradually.
  • Respect scheduled rest days.

Don't

  • Train through significant pain.
  • Use extreme volume to compensate for missed sessions.

📚 Reading

Small consistent sessions compound over the Arc.

Do

  • Read a manageable amount every day.
  • Write down useful ideas.

Don't

  • Turn reading into a meaningless page-count competition.

Everyday nutrition

Build meals around a variety of foods: grains or other carbohydrates, protein-rich foods, vegetables or fruit, and sources of healthy fats. Regular meals and adequate energy support school, activity and recovery.

Recovery
